Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Competitive Exam AI ("the app", "we", "us") is a study platform for Indian competitive exams (NDA, SSC, UPSC, banking, railway, JEE, NEET and others). This page explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: the email address and password you sign up with. Your password is never stored in plain text — it is hashed (scrypt) before being saved.
  • Profile and preferences: your name, chosen exam, preparation level, target exam date and daily study time, entered during onboarding or from your profile.
  • Study activity: the questions you answer, your answers, timing, test attempts and results, bookmarks, notes, and messages you send to the AI Tutor — used to power practice, mistake analysis, revision and your personal recommendations.
  • Uploaded files: images or PDFs you upload to the AI Tutor or paper solver (for example, a photo of a question or question paper), sent to our AI provider to generate a response. These are not stored permanently beyond what is needed to answer your request and keep your chat history.
  • Technical information: IP address and basic request metadata, used only for security purposes such as rate-limiting and abuse prevention.

2. How we use your information

  • To run the core features of the app: practice, tests, revision, mistake tracking, current affairs, the AI Tutor and your personal dashboard.
  • To personalise recommendations based on your accuracy, weak topics and activity.
  • To maintain the security of your account (sessions, login attempts, rate limits).
  • We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your data for advertising.

3. AI processing

Questions you ask the AI Tutor, and any images/PDFs you upload for solving, are sent to our AI provider (currently Google Gemini, with Anthropic as a fallback) to generate a response. These providers process the content to return an answer; we do not control their retention policies beyond what they publish for their API products. Avoid uploading documents containing other people's personal information.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We share data only with:

  • The AI provider(s) above, strictly to generate responses to your requests.
  • Our hosting provider (Railway), which stores the application database and runs the server.
  • Authorities, only if required by law.

5. Data retention

We keep your account and study data for as long as your account is active, so your progress, history and revision schedule remain available to you. You can delete your study history at any time from Profile → Reset preferences (with the "also delete my history" option). To delete your account entirely, contact us using the details below.

6. Your choices

  • You can update your profile and preferences at any time.
  • You can reset your preferences and optionally wipe your answer/test/mistake/bookmark history from the Profile page.
  • You can log out of all devices by changing your password.
  • You can request a copy of your data or full account deletion by emailing us (below).

7. Security

We use password hashing, HTTPS, security headers, cross-site request forgery protection and rate-limiting to protect your account. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

8. Children's privacy

This app is intended for students preparing for competitive exams and is not directed at young children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information without appropriate consent, contact us and we will remove it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. The "Last updated" date above reflects the latest revision. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
